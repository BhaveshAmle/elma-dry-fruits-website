Our story

The ELMA narrative.

ELMA was born from a desire to transform the simple act of sharing premium dry fruits into a refined ritual. We believe that nature’s bounty deserves more than generic packaging; it deserves a stage. By meticulously sourcing current-season harvests from Dubai and global heritage markets, we ensure every nut and raisin gift box meets a standard of crunch and flavor rarely found in ordinary markets.

Our philosophy centers on the 'Keepsake Experience.' Each ELMA selection is housed in reusable glass jars, hand-finished wooden cases, or artisan gold trays. These aren't just containers—they are designed as luxury dry fruit hampers to be integrated into your home long after the contents have been enjoyed, serving as a lasting reminder of a thoughtful gesture.

Whether it is a festival celebration, a milestone wedding, or a corporate token of appreciation, ELMA provides a dry fruit gift box that speaks of quality, health, and effortless sophistication. We invite you to gift not just a snack, but a beautifully presented piece of nature.

Purity & Selection

Every batch is hand-sorted for size and consistency, ensuring only the most premium grades reach our jars.

Sustainable Luxury

Our packaging is 100% plastic-free and crafted for longevity, encouraging reuse and reducing gifting waste.
